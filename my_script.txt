The hardest moments in life rarely arrive with warning. They appear quietly, disguised as ordinary days, until everything changes. Every challenge you face is shaping a stronger, wiser version of yourself. So when the road feels impossible and the future uncertain, remember this: your story is still being written. The chapter you're in today is not the ending. It is the beginning.
